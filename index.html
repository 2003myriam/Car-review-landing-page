Advanced Technology

Ultra-Fast Charging

The Polestar 6 features a 110 kWh battery with an 800 V architecture, allowing a 10–80% charge in record time. Enjoy up to 595 km of range (WLTP) for an uncompromised electric driving experience.

Smart Interface

A central touchscreen with Android Automotive delivers an intuitive, connected interface. Full smartphone integration, smart navigation, and OTA updates keep your system always up to date.

Safe and Assisted Driving

Advanced sensors and cameras enable adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, and emergency braking. The Polestar 6 combines safety and comfort with semi-autonomous assistance on every journey.

Innovation and Elegance

Premium interior with sustainable materials and a bonded aluminum chassis for lightness and rigidity. Adaptive LED lighting and minimalist design create a futuristic and refined experience.
